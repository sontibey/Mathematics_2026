# Exercise 7. A Trigonometric Function

$$
f\prime(x)=\cos x-\sin x=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x=\pi/4,5\pi/4\qquad(0<x<2\pi).
$$

The derivative signs are positive on $(0,\pi/4)$, negative on $(\pi/4,5\pi/4)$, and positive on $(5\pi/4,2\pi)$. The candidate values are

$$
f(0)=1,\quad f(\pi/4)=\sqrt2,\quad f(5\pi/4)=-\sqrt2,\quad f(2\pi)=1.
$$

The absolute maximum is $\sqrt2$ at $\pi/4$ and absolute minimum is $-\sqrt2$ at $5\pi/4$. On the restricted interval, 0 is a one-sided local minimum and $2\pi$ a one-sided local maximum, though neither is an absolute extremum. The identity $f(x)=\sqrt2\sin(x+\pi/4)$ confirms the values.
