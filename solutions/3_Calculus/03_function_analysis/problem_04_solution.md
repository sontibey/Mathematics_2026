# Exercise 4. A Function with Two Vertical Asymptotes

The domain excludes $x=\pm1$. There are no zeros. The function is positive on $(-\infty,-1)$ and $(1,\infty)$ and negative on $(-1,1)$. The horizontal asymptote is $y=0$.

$$
f\prime(x)=\frac{-2x}{(x^2-1)^2}.
$$

It increases on $(-\infty,-1)$ and $(-1,0)$, and decreases on $(0,1)$ and $(1,\infty)$; $(0,-1)$ is a local maximum. At $x=-1$, the left/right limits are $+\infty,-\infty$; at $x=1$ they are $-\infty,+\infty$. These are vertical asymptotes. Intervals separated by a missing domain point must be analyzed individually; monotonicity across a discontinuity does not follow from the derivative sign on the pieces.
