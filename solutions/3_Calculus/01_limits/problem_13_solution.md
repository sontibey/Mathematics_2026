# Exercise 13. Fundamental Trigonometric Limit

Using angles in radians,

$$
\frac{\sin5x}{x}=5\frac{\sin5x}{5x}\longrightarrow5.
$$

$$
\frac{\sin2x}{\sin3x}=\frac23\frac{\sin2x}{2x}\frac{3x}{\sin3x}\longrightarrow\frac23.
$$

The rescaled arguments tend to zero, so every fundamental-limit factor tends to 1; the denominator factor is nonzero near zero apart from the excluded point.
