# Exercise 8. A Square Root in a Function Limit

For $x\ge0$ and $x\ne4$, multiply by $\sqrt x+2$:

$$
\frac{\sqrt x-2}{x-4}=\frac1{\sqrt x+2}\longrightarrow\frac14.
$$

As in Exercise 3, the conjugate converts a troublesome subtraction into an algebraically simpler expression. Here the original form is $0/0$, rather than $\infty-\infty$.
