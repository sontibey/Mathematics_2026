# Exercise 4. Powers and Oscillation

- $(2/3)^n\to0$ since its positive base is smaller than 1; the sequence decreases geometrically.
- $(3/2)^n\to+\infty$ since its base is greater than 1.
- $(-1)^n$ has no limit: its even subsequence is 1 and its odd subsequence is $-1$.
- $(-1)^n/n\to0$ by $-1/n\le(-1)^n/n\le1/n$ and the squeeze theorem. It oscillates with an amplitude tending to zero.
