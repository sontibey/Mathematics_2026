# Exercise 2. Different Growth Rates

$$
\frac{3n+1}{n^2+4}=\frac{3/n+1/n^2}{1+4/n^2}\longrightarrow0.
$$

$$
\frac{2n^3-n}{5n^2+1}=n\frac{2-1/n^2}{5+1/n^2}\longrightarrow+\infty.
$$

For polynomial quotients, a smaller numerator degree gives limit zero; equal degrees give the ratio of leading coefficients; a larger numerator degree gives unbounded magnitude as $n\to\infty$, with eventual sign determined by the leading coefficients. The second expression grows like $2n/5$.
