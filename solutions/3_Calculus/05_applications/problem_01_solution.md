# Exercise 1. Motion — from Position to Velocity

$$
v(t)=s\prime(t)=3t^2-12t+9=3(t-1)(t-3),\qquad a(t)=v\prime(t)=6t-12.
$$

The point stops at $t=1$ and $t=3$. For physical times $t\ge0$, velocity is positive on $[0,1)$ and $(3,\infty)$, and negative on $(1,3)$. It therefore moves in the positive axis direction before 1 and after 3, and in the negative direction between them. It is momentarily at rest at the two transition times.
