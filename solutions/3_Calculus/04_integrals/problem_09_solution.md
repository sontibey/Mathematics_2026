# Exercise 9. Definite Integral and Geometric Area

$$
\int_{-1}^{2}x\,dx=\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_{-1}^{2}=2-\frac12=\frac32.
$$

The part below the axis on $[-1,0]$ is a triangle of area $1/2$; the part above on $[0,2]$ is a triangle of area 2. The definite integral is signed accumulation $2-1/2=3/2$, while geometric area is $2+1/2=5/2$. Equivalently, area is the integral of $|x|$.
