# Exercise 10. The Tangent Line as an Application of the Derivative

$$
f(2)=-1,\qquad f\prime(x)=2x-3,\qquad f\prime(2)=1.
$$

The tangent passes through $(2,-1)$ with slope 1:

$$
y+1=x-2,\qquad y=x-3.
$$

Substituting $x=2$ gives $y=-1$. Also $f(x)-(x-3)=(x-2)^2$, whose value and first derivative vanish at 2, confirming tangency.
