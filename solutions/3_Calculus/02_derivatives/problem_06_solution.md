# Exercise 6. Product Rule and Trigonometry

## Question

For

$$
f(x)=x\sin x+\cos x
$$

compute the derivative and simplify the result. Then compute $f'(0)$ and interpret the direction of the tangent line at that point.

## Solution

$$
f\prime(x)=\sin x+x\cos x-\sin x=x\cos x.
$$

The first two terms come from the product rule for $x\sin x$; the last comes from $\cos x$. Thus $f\prime(0)=0$. Since $f(0)=1$, the tangent is horizontal, with equation $y=1$.
