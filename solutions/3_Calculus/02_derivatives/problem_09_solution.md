# Exercise 9. A Logarithm and the Chain Rule

The logarithm argument $x^2+1$ is positive for every real $x$, and the chain rule gives

$$
f\prime(x)=\frac{2x}{x^2+1}.
$$

The denominator is positive, so the derivative is negative for $x<0$, zero at $x=0$, and positive for $x>0$. The function decreases toward its minimum $f(0)=0$ and then increases; the graph is symmetric about the vertical axis.
