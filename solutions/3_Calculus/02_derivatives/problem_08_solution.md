# Exercise 8. Two Exponential Functions

## Question

For

$$
f(x)=e^{2x-3}+5^x
$$

compute the derivative. Explain why the factor $2$ appears in the first term and why $\ln5$ appears in the second.

## Solution

$$
f\prime(x)=2e^{2x-3}+5^x\ln5.
$$

The factor 2 is the derivative of the inner exponent $2x-3$. For the second term, rewrite $5^x=e^{x\ln5}$; the chain rule supplies $\ln5$, the derivative of $x\ln5$. Both terms are positive for all real $x$.
