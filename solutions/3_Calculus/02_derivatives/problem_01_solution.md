# Exercise 1. Derivative of a Polynomial and the Slope of a Tangent Line

Apply the power rule term by term:

$$
f\prime(x)=15x^4-6x^2+7,\qquad f\prime(0)=7,\qquad f\prime(1)=16.
$$

Both slopes are positive, so the tangents rise from left to right at these points. These statements concern the local tangent slopes, rather than being a complete global monotonicity analysis.
