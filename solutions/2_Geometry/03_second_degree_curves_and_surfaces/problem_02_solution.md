# Exercise 2. Circle by Completing the Square

$$
(x-3)^2-9+(y+2)^2-4-3=0,\qquad (x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=16.
$$

The center is $(3,-2)$ and radius is 4. Two points are $(7,-2)$ and $(-1,-2)$. In canonical form each gives $16+0=16$. In the original form, the residuals are $49+4-42-8-3=0$ and $1+4+6-8-3=0$, respectively.
