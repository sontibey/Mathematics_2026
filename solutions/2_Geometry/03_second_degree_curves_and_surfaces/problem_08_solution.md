# Exercise 8. Sphere

## Question

Reduce

$$
x^2+y^2+z^2-4x+2y-6z-2=0
$$

to canonical form. Find the center and radius. Give two points on the sphere and check them in the equation.

## Solution

$$
(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2+(z-3)^2=16.
$$

The center is $(2,-1,3)$ and radius is 4. Two points are $(6,-1,3)$ and $(-2,-1,3)$. Each has squared distance 16 from the center. In the original equation their residuals are $36+1+9-24-2-18-2=0$ and $4+1+9+8-2-18-2=0$.
