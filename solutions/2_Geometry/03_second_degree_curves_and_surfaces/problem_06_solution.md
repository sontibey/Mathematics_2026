# Exercise 6. Shifted Ellipse

$$
4(x-2)^2+9(y+1)^2=36,\qquad \frac{(x-2)^2}{9}+\frac{(y+1)^2}{4}=1.
$$

The center is $(2,-1)$, with horizontal semiaxis 3 and vertical semiaxis 2. At the center the original left side is $16+9-32-18-11=-36$. This agrees with the rewritten residual $4(x-2)^2+9(y+1)^2-36=-36$. The center is inside the ellipse, not on its boundary.
