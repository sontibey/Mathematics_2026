# Exercise 9. Ellipsoid and Its Cross-Sections

The semiaxes are 3 along $x$, 2 along $y$, and 4 along $z$. At $z=0$ the section is the ellipse $x^2/9+y^2/4=1$. For $|c|<4$,

$$
\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1-\frac{c^2}{16}.
$$

The semiaxes are $3\sqrt{1-c^2/16}$ and $2\sqrt{1-c^2/16}$, so the sections shrink symmetrically as $|c|$ increases. At $c=\pm4$ each section is a point; for $|c|>4$ it is empty.
