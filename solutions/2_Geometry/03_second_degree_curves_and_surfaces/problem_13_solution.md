# Exercise 13. Intersection of a Sphere and a Plane

At $z=3$, substitution into the sphere gives $x^2+y^2=16$. The intersection is a circle in the plane $z=3$, with center $(0,0,3)$ and radius 4.

For general $z=c$, the equation is $x^2+y^2=25-c^2$. If $|c|<5$, it is a circle of radius $\sqrt{25-c^2}$ and center $(0,0,c)$. If $|c|=5$, it is the single point $(0,0,c)$. If $|c|>5$, no real intersection exists.
