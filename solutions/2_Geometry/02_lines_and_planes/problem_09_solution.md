# Exercise 9. Distances

The distance to $n\cdot x=d$ is $|n\cdot P-d|/\|n\|$, the absolute normal component of the displacement.

$$
d(P,L)=\frac{|3(2)+4(3)-10|}{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}=\frac85.
$$

$$
d(Q,\Pi)=\frac{|2(2)-2(-1)+3-4|}{\sqrt{2^2+(-2)^2+1^2}}=\frac53.
$$

Both formulas divide the equation residual by the length of its normal, making the distance independent of how the same equation is scaled.
