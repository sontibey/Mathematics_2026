# Exercise 2. A Line in Space

## Question

Given

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2).
$$

State a point on the line and its direction vector, check whether $P=(4,3,-1)$ lies on $L$, and find the point corresponding to $t=-1$.

## Solution

A point is $(2,-1,3)$ and the direction is $(1,2,-2)$. For $P=(4,3,-1)$, the first coordinate forces $t=2$. The other two coordinates then give $-1+4=3$ and $3-4=-1$, so $P\in L$. At $t=-1$, the point is $(1,-3,5)$.
