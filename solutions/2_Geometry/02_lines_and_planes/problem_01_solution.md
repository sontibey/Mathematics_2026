# Exercise 1. Line Through Two Points

A direction vector is $B-A=(3,-3)$, which can be simplified to $v=(1,-1)$. A normal is $n=(1,1)$ because $n\cdot v=0$.

$$
(x,y)=(1,2)+t(1,-1),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

The normal equation is $n\cdot((x,y)-(1,2))=0$, hence $x+y-3=0$. The points satisfy $1+2-3=0$ and $4-1-3=0$; the parametric values are $t=0$ and $t=3$.
