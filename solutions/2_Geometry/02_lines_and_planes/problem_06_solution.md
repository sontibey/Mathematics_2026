# Exercise 6. Angle Between Planes

The normals $n_1=(1,1,1)$ and $n_2=(1,-1,0)$ are not proportional, so the planes are not parallel. The acute or right dihedral angle satisfies

$$
\cos\phi=\frac{|n_1\cdot n_2|}{\|n_1\|\|n_2\|}=\frac{|1-1|}{\sqrt3\sqrt2}=0.
$$

Thus $\phi=\pi/2=90^\circ$. The normals encode the plane orientations; translating a plane changes its constant term but not its angle with another plane.
