# Exercise 7. A Line Relative to a Plane

## Question

For

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,-1,0),\qquad \Pi:x+y+z=2,
$$

determine whether the line intersects the plane at one point, is parallel to it, or lies in it. Explain the role of the dot product of the direction vector and the normal vector.

## Solution

Here $v=(1,-1,0)$ and $n=(1,1,1)$, so $n\cdot v=0$. The direction is parallel to the plane. The base point $(1,1,0)$ satisfies $x+y+z=2$, and every point has sum $(1+t)+(1-t)+0=2$. Thus the entire line lies in the plane, rather than being disjoint or meeting it once.
