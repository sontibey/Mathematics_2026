# Exercise 3. Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector

The normal is perpendicular to every direction within the plane. Use $n\cdot((x,y,z)-P)=0$:

$$
2(x-1)+(y+2)-(z-3)=0,\qquad 2x+y-z+3=0.
$$

At $P$, the left side is $2-2-3+3=0$. For any two points on the plane, subtracting their equations gives zero dot product of their difference with $n$, confirming normality.
