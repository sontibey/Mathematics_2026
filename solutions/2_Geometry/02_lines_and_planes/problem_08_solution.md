# Exercise 8. Intersection Point of a Line and a Plane

Substitution gives $(1+t)+2t+(2-t)=4$, hence $3+2t=4$ and $t=1/2$. The intersection is

$$
Q=(3/2,1,3/2).
$$

Its coordinate sum is $4$, and substituting $t=1/2$ into the parametric line gives exactly $Q$. Uniqueness follows because $n\cdot v=(1,1,1)\cdot(1,2,-1)=2\ne0$.
