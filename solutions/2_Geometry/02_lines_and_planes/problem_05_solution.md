# Exercise 5. Relative Position of Two Lines

The directions $(1,2,1)$ and $(0,1,-1)$ are not proportional, so the lines are not parallel. Equality of the first coordinates requires $1+t=2$, hence $t=1$. Equality of the second then requires $1+2t=s$, hence $s=3$. But the third coordinates are $t=1$ and $1-s=-2$, which disagree. There is no intersection, so the lines are skew.
