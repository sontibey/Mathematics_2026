# Exercise 4. Plane Through Three Points

Two in-plane vectors are $B-A=(-1,1,0)$ and $C-A=(-1,0,1)$. Their cross product is $(1,1,1)$, a nonzero normal. Therefore

$$
(1,1,1)\cdot((x,y,z)-(1,0,0))=0,\qquad x+y+z=1.
$$

All three given points have coordinate sum 1. The nonzero cross product also verifies that they are noncollinear and determine a unique plane.
