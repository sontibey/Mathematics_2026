# Exercise 5. Matrix Times Vector

$$
Ax=\begin{pmatrix}
2(4)-1(2) \\
1(4)+3(2) \\
\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The vector entries weight the corresponding columns of $A$:

$$
Ax=4\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix}+2\begin{pmatrix}
-1 \\
3 \\
\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}
8 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}
-2 \\
6 \\
\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The column combination agrees with the row–column calculation.
