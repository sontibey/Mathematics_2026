# Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2×2 Matrix

For a $2\times2$ matrix, the determinant is $ad-bc$.

$$
D_1=3(4)-(-2)(5)=22,\qquad D_2=1(14)-7(2)=0.
$$

The first matrix is invertible because its determinant is nonzero. The second is not invertible: its second row is twice its first, confirming the zero determinant.
