# Exercise 6. Solving a System Using the Inverse

Here $\det A=6-1=5\ne0$. Hence

$$
A^{-1}=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -1 \\
-1 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad x=A^{-1}b=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}
15-7 \\
-5+14 \\
\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}
8/5 \\
9/5 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Substitution gives $2(8/5)+9/5=5$ and $8/5+3(9/5)=7$, as required.
