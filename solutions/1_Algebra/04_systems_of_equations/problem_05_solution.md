# Exercise 5. Row Echelon Form and Number of Solutions

There are two pivot columns among three variables, with no contradiction row. Thus one variable is free. Put $z=s$; the second row gives $y=1-2s$, and the first gives $x=3-2y+z=1+5s$.

$$
(x,y,z)=(1+5s,1-2s,s),\qquad s\in\mathbb R.
$$

There are infinitely many solutions. Substitution yields $y+2z=1$ and $x+2y-z=3$ identically.
