# Exercise 12. Homogeneous System

Use $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ to obtain a zero row, and $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_1$ to obtain $-3y+3z=0$. Thus $y=z=s$ and the first equation gives $x=-s$.

$$
(x,y,z)=s(-1,1,1),\qquad s\in\mathbb R.
$$

Substitution gives $-s+2s-s=0$ and $-s-s+2s=0$; the second original equation is twice the first. Every homogeneous system has the zero solution because multiplying any matrix by the zero vector yields the zero right-hand side.
