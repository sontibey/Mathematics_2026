# Exercise 4. Infinitely Many Solutions

Use $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ and $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_1$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & 1 & \mid & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \mid & 0 \\
0 & -2 & 0 & \mid & -2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Thus $y=1$ and $x+z=1$. Choose the free variable $z=s$:

$$
(x,y,z)=(1-s,1,s),\qquad s\in\mathbb R.
$$

There are infinitely many solutions. At $s=0$, $(1,1,0)$ gives left sides $2,4,0$, matching the required right sides. More generally, substitution works for every $s$.
