# Exercise 10. A Polynomial Through Three Points

The points impose $c=1$, $a+b+c=3$, and $4a+2b+c=9$. Hence $a+b=2$ and $4a+2b=8$. Subtracting twice the former gives $2a=4$, so $a=2$, $b=0$, $c=1$.

$$
p(x)=2x^2+1.
$$

The checks are $p(0)=1$, $p(1)=3$, and $p(2)=9$. Elimination determines every coefficient uniquely.
