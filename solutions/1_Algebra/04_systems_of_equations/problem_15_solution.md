# Exercise 15. Classifying Three Systems

**System 1.** Subtract equation 1 from equation 2: $-2y=-2$. Thus $y=1$, $x=1$; there are two pivots and exactly one solution.

**System 2.** Subtract twice equation 1 from equation 2: $0=0$. One independent equation remains, giving infinitely many solutions $(x,y)=(2-s,s)$.

**System 3.** The same elimination gives $0=1$. This contradiction means there are no solutions. These outcomes follow directly from the final rows, without further elimination.
