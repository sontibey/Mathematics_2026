# Exercise 9. A System from a Sum Condition

## Question

Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying

$$
x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.
$$

Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

Subtract twice $x+y=10$ from $2x+5y=32$: $3y=12$, so $y=4$ and $x=6$. Check: $6+4=10$ and $12+20=32$. The lines have distinct slopes $-1$ and $-2/5$, so they intersect in exactly one point. Equivalently, the coefficient determinant is $5-2=3\ne0$.
