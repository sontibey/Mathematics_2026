# Exercise 1. From a System to a Matrix

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 1 \\
1 & 3 & -2 \\
3 & 1 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad x=\begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad b=\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
1 \\
7 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then $Ax=b$. Row 1 yields $2x-y+z=4$, row 2 yields $x+3y-2z=1$, and row 3 yields $3x+y+z=7$, recovering precisely the original equations.
